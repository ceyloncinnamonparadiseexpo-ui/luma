## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the sensory rhythm of Sri Lanka’s southern coast: salt air, tropical flora, warm sun-baked parchment, and spice-trade heritage. It targets discerning global travelers, wellness retreat seekers, and modern coastal wanderers who prioritize thoughtful luxury, authentic vernacular character, and serene hospitality over generic corporate opulence.

The aesthetic philosophy fuses **Refined Coastal Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Editorial**. The interface balances sunlit negative space with deeply grounded organic hues. Visual layouts evoke high-end boutique travel monographs and leisurely morning journals, rejecting clinical SaaS sharpness in favor of relaxed breathing room, textured materiality, and poised, poetic typography.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in the natural terroir of Weligama Bay:
- **Ocean Blue (`#1B4965`, `#62B6CB`):** Deep oceanic depths anchoring key actions, navigation bars, and primary interactive states, balanced by coastal seafoam accents for highlights and active tab indicators.
- **Lush Leafy Green (`#2D5A27`, `#4F772D`):** Rich jungle canopy tones reserved for nature-inspired tags, eco-initiatives, wellness programs, and seasonal botanical highlights.
- **Cinnamon Brown (`#8C4A2F`, `#B85D36`, `#5C2C16`):** Evocative spice tones for secondary actions, booking accents, editorial pull quotes, and rich hover states.
- **Warm Sand & Parchment (`#FDFBF7`, `#F4EBD9`, `#E6DCB8`):** The foundation of the visual experience. The canvas rejects harsh pure white `#FFFFFF` in favor of `#FDFBF7`, with `#F4EBD9` and `#E6DCB8` forming soft organic surfaces, structural dividers, and warm backdrops.

Text contrast uses deep espresso-charcoal (`#1F1D1A`) on parchment surfaces to maintain warmth and effortless legibility.

## Typography

The type system creates an intentional dialogue between the classical grace of **Playfair Display** and the crisp, approachable modernism of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Editorial Headings:** Playfair Display introduces literary charm and timeless poise. Headings larger than 30px adopt subtle negative tracking for a compact editorial lockup.
- **Body & Functional Text:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides generous counter-spaces, smooth legibility at small sizes, and clean structural reading for suite descriptions, pricing breakdowns, itineraries, and menus.
- **Micro-Copy & Badges:** `label-caps` must always be rendered in full uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.12em`), creating a premium, tactile stamp effect for room categories, curation tags, and location coordinates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on a relaxed fluid-responsive grid system designed to simulate the unhurried luxury of an open-air pavilion:

- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column grid with a maximum content container width of 1320px, framed by expansive `margin-desktop` (64px) to preserve calm breathing margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with 24px gutters, adapting editorial sidebars into sequential rhythm sections.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 20px outer margin, ensuring touch targets remain comfortable while visual cards stretch gracefully edge-to-edge with inset padding.

Vertical rhythm favors generous sectional breaks (`space-xl`), allowing architectural imagery and resort amenities to breathe without visual congestion.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this design system avoids aggressive synthetic drop shadows. Instead, it relies on warm, sun-diffused ambient light and natural paper layering:

- **Ambient Shading:** Elevated elements cast low-opacity shadows tinted with deep cinnamon-sand undertones (`rgba(92, 44, 22, 0.07)` with blur radii from 12px to 24px) rather than cold synthetic black.
- **Tonal Stepping:** Surfaces stack naturally: the primary canvas `#FDFBF7` rests below parchment surface containers (`#F4EBD9`), which in turn hold bright floating cards or modals.
- **Natural Borders:** Subtle 1px borders colored in soft sand-ochre (`#E6DCB8` at 60% opacity) define structural cards and separators, evoking deck timber joinery and woven textiles.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates soft, welcoming geometry inspired by beach pebbles, ocean-smoothed sea glass, and curved archways of tropical colonial architecture:

- Standard interactive controls and input containers use an 8px radius (`roundedness: 2`).
- Media cards and imagery containers use 16px (`rounded-lg`), providing an inviting, tactile softness to photography.
- Experience tags, amenity badges, and floating status chips use full pill geometry (`9999px`) to create hand-crafted tactile seals.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Ocean Blue):** Deep ocean blue background (`#1B4965`) with warm sand typography (`#FDFBF7`), 8px border-radius, and generous horizontal padding (`space-lg`). On hover, transitions smoothly to Cinnamon Brown (`#8C4A2F`) with a subtle lift.
- **Secondary Action (Warm Sand Outline):** Transparent background with a 1.5px solid border in `#1B4965`, typography in `#1B4965`. On hover, fills with a gentle sand wash (`#F4EBD9`).
- **Editorial Text Action:** Playfair Display medium weight paired with an animated terracotta underline (`#B85D36`) and a right arrow glyph for exploration cues.

### Chips & Badges
- **Amenity Badges:** Pill-shaped capsules with sand background (`#F4EBD9`), 1px outline in `#E6DCB8`, using uppercase `label-caps` in `#5C2C16`.
- **Wellness & Nature Tags:** Leafy green tint (`#4F772D` at 12% opacity) with solid green typography (`#2D5A27`).

### Cards (Suites & Experiences)
- Enclosed in an off-white background (`#FFFFFF` with warm parchment undertones) over the canvas, surrounded by a 1px border of `#E6DCB8`.
- 16px border-radius with overflow hidden on image headers. Imagery employs warm, desaturated color grading.
- Includes micro-headers in Cinnamon Brown uppercase text, Playfair room titles, and bottom booking price docks separated by subtle hairline rules.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background filled in `#FDFBF7` with a 1px boundary in `#E6DCB8`.
- Focus states shift the border to Ocean Blue (`#1B4965`) alongside a warm ambient focus halo (`rgba(27, 73, 101, 0.15)`).
- Placeholder copy rendered in warm muted mocha (`#8C827A`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Rounded squares (for checkboxes) and circles (for radios) framed in 1.5px `#1B4965`.
- Active state fills with `#1B4965` displaying an ivory-white checkmark or central pip.

### Specialized Hospitality Components
- **Booking Engine Bar:** A horizontal floating console utilizing parchment layers, dividing dates, guest counts, and villa categories with delicate vertical sand dividers.
- **Tide & Weather Widget:** An unobtrusive ambient status card featuring minimal sun/ocean iconography, tide heights, and surf conditions rendered in clean coastal tones.
- **Vernacular Menu Drawer:** A full-bleed parchment menu panel with graceful editorial typography, reminiscent of hand-printed resort dining cards.
